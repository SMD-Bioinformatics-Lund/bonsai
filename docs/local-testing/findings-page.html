Scope, evidence and next steps

This is a snapshot of the first representative synthetic pass, completed on 7 November 2026. Findings remain open. After fixes, the failing cases should be rerun on fresh input, followed by the passing workflows that share the changed code.

  • 479 report parses were validated. The fixtures are parser-compatible synthetic reports, not outputs from a completed JASEN run.
  • The eight assay paths include both kpneumoniae and klebsiella; they are not eight distinct species. Generic streptococcus and staphylococcus examples were included.
  • The reference is 100 kb and the BAM contains 20 synthetic reads. IGV access and presentation were checked; production performance and biological accuracy were not measured.
  • LDAP/SSO, production infrastructure and real clinical datasets were not tested. The uploader-only role was denied frontend access, consistent with its API-only permissions.
  • TB003 was changed by the UI pass: two curations, one comment and passed QC were saved. Administrator creation/editing could not reach a successful path because of the recorded failures.
